Solid-state chemistry in action

We study how solids change when they react. Our research themes seek the same goal from different angles: if we can understand why chemistry changes, we can direct it. X-rays let us watch it happen. Our lab spans UIC Chemistry and Argonne National Laboratory.

What we work on

Our work follows four connected questions about the chemistry of solids, and the answers feed into electrochemical technologies. Examples include batteries, electrocatalysis, metal manufacturing and microelectronics. Our Principal Investigator is Jordi Cabana.

Research

Our work centers on ionic conduction, redox and intercalation chemistry in solids. We look for new phases, or assemble known ones into multifunctional architectures, and we build chemical imaging and mapping methods in 2D and 3D, increasingly supported by machine learning and AI.
